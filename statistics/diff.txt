why sample variance divided by n-1 We divide by n - 1 instead of n in the sample variance formula—a adjustment known as Bessel's Correction—to make the sample variance an unbiased estimator of the true population variance. Unbiased Estimation: If you divide by n, your calculated sample variances tend to be slightly smaller than the true population variance on average (a biased estimate). Dividing by n - 1 boosts the result just enough to correct this underestimation
